Mathematics: Immersive Geometry

Build, measure, and solve in a world where geometric shapes come to life.

Module Overview

Go beyond the textbook and step into a world of shapes, angles, and dimensions. This module allows you to physically manipulate 2D and 3D shapes, intuitively understanding concepts like area, volume, and surface area. Deconstruct complex objects, visualize theorems in action, and see how geometry applies to the real world in a fully interactive environment.

Key Learning Objectives:

  • Calculate the area and perimeter of 2D shapes.
  • Calculate the volume and surface area of 3D shapes like prisms, cylinders, and spheres.
  • Visually prove and apply the Pythagorean theorem.
  • Understand and identify conic sections by slicing through 3D objects.
