History: A Day in Ancient Rome

Travel back in time and walk the bustling streets of the Roman Empire at its peak.

Module Overview

Forget dusty textbooks. This module transports you to 100 A.D. to experience the Roman Forum firsthand. Listen to the arguments of senators, barter for goods in the market, visit the majestic Colosseum, and learn about the daily routines, social structure, and engineering marvels of the Roman Empire from the citizens themselves.

Key Learning Objectives:

  • Identify key architectural styles and buildings in the Roman Forum.
  • Understand the social hierarchy of Roman society.
  • Learn about Roman governance, law, and commerce.
  • Experience the cultural significance of landmarks like the Colosseum.
