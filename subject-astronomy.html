Astronomy: A Walk on Mars

Step onto the surface of the Red Planet and become a planetary explorer.

Module Overview

Experience the Red Planet like never before. In this module, you will land on Mars, collect geological samples from the Victoria Crater, analyze the thin Martian atmosphere, and witness a breathtaking blue sunset. This simulation is built on real NASA topographical data for an unparalleled sense of realism.

Key Learning Objectives:

  • Understand the geological features of Mars, including craters, canyons, and polar ice caps.
  • Learn about the composition of the Martian atmosphere and its differences from Earth's.
  • Experience the effects of lower gravity on movement and object interaction.
  • Identify key landmarks from past and present Mars missions.
